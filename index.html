For CFOs

Self-funding solves cost. It doesn't solve predictability.

The challenge

Predictability

For CFOs self-funding medical benefit plans, the vexing problem isn't the average cost — it's the variance. One catastrophic claim year turns a budgeted line item into an unbudgeted event, and annual renewals reprice the risk every twelve months.

  • Claims volatility lands directly on the P&L
  • Annual stop-loss renewals reset the cost basis
  • Reserves sit idle, tied up against a worst case
The solution

A 3-year level-funded premium

WorldVest Re, through its stablecoin / reinsurance model, offers a level-funded premium held for three years — one fixed, budgetable number across the full term instead of a renewal cycle you can't forecast.

3  years Level-funded premium term

The reinsurance layer absorbs the volatility. The digital dollar makes the reserves behind it liquid, provable, and same-day — so the guarantee is funded, not just promised.

Fund the structure

Plan sponsors, captives, and reinsurers capitalize the program in WVDC. Reserves are visible on-chain, attested monthly, and never leave regulated custody.

Move between parties

Premium cessions, collateral top-ups, and inter-entity transfers settle in seconds, 24/7 — not on banking hours, and not by correspondent chains.

Pay the claim

When losses hit, redeem to U.S. dollars at par or swap to USDC. No lockups, no withdrawal windows. The money is there because it never stopped being money.

Reserved

Every WVDC is backed one-for-one by cash and short-term U.S. treasuries, held at regulated custodians and segregated from WorldVest Re's own operating funds. It is designed to stay at exactly one dollar.

Verified
