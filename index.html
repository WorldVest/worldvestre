The model

Risk capital that clears like cash, because it is cash.

In a traditional program, money is the slow part: premium wires, collateral postings, letters of credit, claims funding across borders. WorldVest Re replaces the idle, in-transit dollar with a reserved digital one.

Every layer of the tower holds the same instrument: a fully reserved digital dollar, auditable on-chain, redeemable when claims come due.

Fund the structure

Cedents, captives, and reinsurers capitalize the program in WVDC. Reserves are visible on-chain, attested monthly, and never leave regulated custody.

Move between parties

Premium cessions, collateral top-ups, and inter-entity transfers settle in seconds, 24/7 — not on banking hours, and not by correspondent chains.

Pay the claim

When losses hit, redeem to U.S. dollars at par or swap to USDC. No lockups, no withdrawal windows. The money is there because it never stopped being money.

How your money is held

Every WVDC is a claim on a real dollar.

Will it hold its value?

Reserved

Every WVDC is backed one-for-one by cash and short-term U.S. treasuries, held at regulated custodians and segregated from WorldVest Re's own operating funds. It is designed to stay at exactly one dollar.

Who is holding my money?

Verified

Reserves sit in segregated accounts at named third-party custodians — not with us alone. Independent accountants attest to reserves on a published schedule, and our contracts are audited and continuously monitored.

Can I redeem at par, any time?

Redeemable

Redeem WVDC 1:1 for U.S. dollars or swap to USDC at par, any time. No lockups, no withdrawal windows — your money is available when claims come due.
